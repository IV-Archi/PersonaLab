## Slide 2: Macro Problem: The "Cheating AI" Epidemic (Macro Perspective)
* **Visual Layout:** High-contrast statistics showing large numbers. Minimalist graphs showing the growth of homework copying.

* **Speaker Script (0:30 - 1:15):**

We are facing a future where students graduate with grades, but without real understanding."

## Slide 3: Micro Problem: The Homework Trap (Micro Perspective)
* **Visual Layout:** Side-by-side split screen. Left side: A photo of "Temirlan", a 14-year-old student from Almaty. Right side: A diagram illustrating the vicious feedback loop of copy-paste study.
* **Slide Text:**
  ## Meet Temirlan: The Copy-Paste Cycle
  * **The Trigger:** Struggles with Algebra homework; parent cannot afford private tutoring.
  * **The Shortcut:** Inputs the homework sheet into ChatGPT. Copies the result.
  * **Secondary Impact:**
    * Passes homework checks with 100% scores.
    * Fails class exams due to lack of concept mastery.
    * Develops test anxiety, feels demotivated, and loses trust in his own abilities.
* **Speaker Script (1:15 - 2:00):**
  > "How does this affect an individual? Meet Temirlan, a 14-year-old student from Almaty. When Temirlan gets stuck on a hard math question, he has no support. His parents cannot afford a private tutor. So, he uploads it to a standard LLM, copies the answer, and hands it in. The immediate problem is solved. But the secondary problems are severe: he passes his homework but fails his exams, loses confidence, and becomes dependent on the AI crutch. He isn't learning; he's translating."
* **Rubric Factor Alignment:** Factor 2 (Micro perspective, individual/family impact, secondary problems).

## Slide 4: Our Approach: AI That Teaches, Not Just Answers
* **Visual Layout:** Active UI screenshot of Persona Lab's Tutor page showing options like "Subject Selection", "Tutor Mode: Explain Simply", "Guardrails: Guided Mode", and "Quick Actions".
* **Slide Text:**
  ## Persona Lab: The Solution
  * **Interactive Step-by-Step AI:** Breaks down complex subjects without giving the final answer.
  * **Localized & Multilingual:** Works fluently in **Kazakh, Russian, and English**.
  * **Pedagogical Guardrails:**
    * *Guided Study Mode:* Refuses to write essays or complete solutions; provides hints instead.
    * *Verification Indicator:* Appends `[Verify]` to facts requiring external verification.
* **Speaker Script (2:00 - 2:45):**
  > "Persona Lab is our approach to fixing this. We built a structured AI tutoring ecosystem that acts like an expert human coach. It works in English, Russian, and Kazakh. Most importantly, it has built-in pedagogical guardrails: when a student asks it to write an essay or solve a math question, the AI refuses to write the final copy. Instead, it guides the student step-by-step with hints, encouraging them to find the answer themselves."
* **Rubric Factor Alignment:** Factor 3 (Explain the approach and how the proposed solution works).

## Slide 8: Business, Operations & Sustainability Model
* **Visual Layout:** High-contrast graphic showing the hybrid B2B/B2G revenue streams and operating costs.
* **Slide Text:**
  ## Business & Sustainability Model
  * **B2C (Freemium):** Free core features (AI Tutor, daily practice). Premium ($4.99/mo) for advanced deep-dives, unlimited custom paths, and deep analytics.
  * **B2B (School SaaS):** Licensing fee ($1.50 per student/year) for school dashboards, auto-assignment tools, and class analytics.
  * **B2G (Rural Educational Equity):** Government/State licensing grants to deploy Persona Lab in rural school districts lacking subject-matter teachers.
  * **Cost Structure:** Server costs optimized via lightweight AI models (`gpt-4o-mini`).
* **Speaker Script (5:00 - 5:45):**
  > "How is this sustainable? We utilize a hybrid business model. While the basic app is free for all students to ensure accessibility, we charge a B2B SaaS license of $1.50 per student/year to private and public schools for our advanced analytics dashboard. Furthermore, we target B2G grants to deploy the system in rural schools where there is a critical shortage of qualified subject-matter teachers."
* **Rubric Factor Alignment:** Factor 7 (Business / operations / sustainability model benefiting all).
