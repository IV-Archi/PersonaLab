## Slide 3: Scope of the Problem (Macro & Micro)
* **Visual Asset:** [student_learning_tech.png](file:///d:/antigravity/projects/PersonaLab/persona-lab-app/public/assets/student_learning_tech.png) showing localized student study, plus a clean demographic map overlay of Kazakhstan regions.
* **Slide Text:**
  ## The Learning Crisis & The Tutoring Gap
  * **Macro Crisis**: Over **3.5 million students** in Kazakhstan. The Ministry of Education reports a severe deficit of subject-matter teachers in rural areas.
  * **The AI Epidemic**: **82% of high school students** admit to using generative AI directly to copy homework answers, skipping critical cognitive steps.
  * **Financial Disparity**: Private tutoring averages **15,000 KZT ($33 USD) per hour**, leaving low-income and rural families completely excluded.
  * **Secondary Impact**: Failing test scores, classroom demotivation, and deep student anxiety.
* **Template Mapping:** 2. Provide Scope of Problem (numbers - how many people, a map, regions, how bad is the problem).
* **Rubric Mapping:** Factor 2 (Explain the problem from a macro perspective and micro/individual/family perspective, target customer segment).
* **Speaker Script (1:20 - 2:20):**
  > "Let's examine the numbers. Kazakhstan has over 3.5 million school students. In urban areas like Astana and Almaty, wealthy families hire tutors. But in rural regions, students lack both subject-matter teachers and the financial means for private support. 
  > 
  > This has created a macro-level educational crisis. When students get stuck, they turn to standard LLMs. Because these models are designed for fast task-completion, they give the answer instantly. The micro-level result? Students pass their homework with 100% scores, but fail their midterms, lose academic confidence, and develop chronic test anxiety. This is a learning regression that affects the core of our educational pipeline. Our target customer segment is the millions of middle-to-low-income students who need high-quality guided instruction but cannot afford it."

## Slide 4: Introduce "Our Solution" & Value Proposition
* **Visual Asset:** [screenshot_home_pricing.png](file:///d:/antigravity/projects/PersonaLab/persona-lab-app/public/assets/screenshot_home_pricing.png) alongside [screenshot_signup_modal.png](file:///d:/antigravity/projects/PersonaLab/persona-lab-app/public/assets/screenshot_signup_modal.png)
* **Slide Text:**
  ## Persona Lab: Interactive Tutoring Ecosystem
  * **Pedagogical Guardrails**: The AI acts as a scaffolded learning tutor—refusing to write essays or answers, instead offering guiding questions.
  * **Localized & Multilingual**: Fully operational in **Kazakh, Russian, and English**.
  * **Dynamic Signup Customization**: Custom registration paths for Students (School/Uni), Teachers, and General Users to align learning content.
  * **Ethics and Trust**: Verification warnings highlight facts requiring external checking.
* **Template Mapping:** 3. Introduce "Our Solution" (Logo / slogan, Value Proposition, Features + capabilities).
* **Rubric Mapping:** Factor 3 (Value created for customers/stakeholders), Factor 10 (Clarity of technology).
* **Speaker Script (2:20 - 3:10):**
  > "Our solution is Persona Lab. Here is how we create value. We have built-in pedagogical guardrails: when a student prompts the AI to 'write my biology essay' or 'solve this equation,' the model refuses. Instead, it responds: 'I can't write that for you, but let's break down the thesis statement together.' 
  > 
  > It is fully localized in Kazakh, Russian, and English. We have created a dynamic signup flow that adapts the dashboard depending on whether you are a school student, university student, or teacher. We also address LLM hallucination: whenever the AI presents factual data, it appends a `[Verify]` warning badge, teaching students media literacy and academic integrity from day one."

## Slide 6: Business, Operations & Sustainability Model
* **Visual Asset:** [teacher_analytics_concept.png](file:///d:/antigravity/projects/PersonaLab/persona-lab-app/public/assets/teacher_analytics_concept.png) illustrating clean diagnostic charts, showing high financial feasibility.
* **Slide Text:**
  ## Operations, Cost Structure & Revenue Streams
  * **Operating Cost Optimization**: Server queries run on highly optimized, fast models (`gpt-4o-mini`), costing less than **$0.01 per student/month**.
  * **Revenue Stream 1: B2C Freemium**
    * *Free Tier*: Access to core AI Tutor & Practice.
    * *Premium ($4.99/mo)*: Unlimited AI tutor queries, advanced career path advising, extra credits.
  * **Revenue Stream 2: B2B School SaaS**
    * *$1.50 per student/year* for private/public schools to access teacher dashboards, class diagnostics, and auto-generated homework.
  * **Revenue Stream 3: B2G Partnerships**
    * State contracts to deploy the platform in rural schools lacking qualified subject-matter teachers.
* **Template Mapping:** 5. Business / Logic (How does your venture function? How does it fit in current setting? Stakeholders, market potential, revenue/cost, customer relationships, customer channels, key).
* **Rubric Mapping:** Factor 6 (Business/operations/sustainability model), Factor 7 (Scale-up strategy & projections).
* **Speaker Script (4:10 - 5:10):**
  > "How does our venture function and sustain itself? We operate on a highly efficient hybrid B2B/B2G business model. Our operations are cheap: by utilizing cached, lightweight AI models, our API costs are less than one cent per student per month. 
  > 
  > For individual users, we offer a Freemium model. The core features are 100% free to maintain accessibility, while advanced features, like unlimited tutor queries and dynamic career paths, cost $4.99/month. 
  > 
  > For schools, we license our dashboard for $1.50 per student/year, giving teachers automated diagnostics and grading features that save them up to 10 hours a week. Finally, we target B2G contracts with the Ministry of Education to deploy the system in rural schools, converting a public educational deficit into a high-impact, cost-effective digital solution."
